How to play

Open Ingenium, go to How to Play, and run the tutorial. Tap a tile to rotate it 90°. Power flows from the lightning generators until every bulb is lit. The board wraps: top joins bottom, left joins right. You have 90 seconds.

No ads, no purchases

Ingenium is free. There are no in-app purchases and no advertisements. You can play every level without signing in.

Leaderboard

Each level’s best score stays on your device. Play Games is optional. If you sign in with Google, only the sum of those records is sent, as one overall leaderboard. There is no separate online board for each level.
